Explore Your Psychic Potential with These Essential Reads

Zan Dean
Aug 22, 2024 · 6 min read

Last year, I had the privilege of creating a Psychic Development Course. It was a powerful experience to step into the role of teacher, honoring the skills and methods I've honed over my years of practice. I'm incredibly proud of the effort and intention that went into the course, and it drew the perfect group of students.

During my recent hiatus from psychic practice, I realized the importance of being patient and specific about when to teach the course again. Having a suitable space and timing is integral to the experience for my students and myself as a teacher. When the time is right, I will announce the next session. Still, until then, I've compiled a list of highly recommended books for anyone seeking to develop their psychic abilities and embark on a journey of spiritual growth.

Choosing the Right Resources for to Develop Your Psychic Potential

Countless books on psychic development exist, but not all are created equal. Developing your psychic abilities isn't just about opening your third eye; it's also about cultivating critical thinking and wisdom. Without these, the information you gather could be meaningless—or worse, a projection of stray intentions. Below are my top five book recommendations to help you build a solid, well-rounded foundation for your psychic practice.

Top 5 Books to Develop Your Psychic Abilities

The Silva Mind Control Method 

by Jose Silva

This book takes on the task of unraveling the power of the mind, the brain, and consciousness. Using the science of brain wave frequencies, Jose Silva has developed a method of hacking into our subconscious and utilizing the resting power of our minds for the good of ourselves and others. While the book is not technically in a psychic context, there is no better foundation builder than the meditation techniques. Use this book to develop control over your mind and better control over your psychic faculties. There is also an in-depth audiobook course with over 60 hours of content. I recommend the book foremost, but if you have the time and dedication to take the course, it will improve your Life. This book delves into the power of the mind, brain, and consciousness using the science of brainwave frequencies. While it's not specifically a psychic book, its meditation techniques are foundational for gaining control over your mind and, by extension, your psychic faculties.

You Are Psychic: The Art of Clairvoyant Reading and Healing 

by Deborah Lynne Katz

Debra does an incredible job of supporting practitioners in building a solid foundation for their psychic practice. Through time-tested techniques taught in psychic schools and gatekept traditions, Debra shares lessons in power and uplifts the reader, enhancing the confidence and skills of the fledgling psychic. This book is also a powerful refresher for adept practitioners. Build a secure and disciplined practice in psychic protocols, develop clairs, and expand your practice. She has additional books in this series: Extraordinary Psychic, Freeing the Genie Within, and Associative Remote Viewing. Deborah Katz's book is a must-read for anyone serious about building a secure and disciplined psychic practice. It's perfect for beginners and advanced practitioners looking to refine their skills and expand their psychic abilities.


Anatomy of the Spirit 

by Carolyn Myss

Carolyn Myss is a powerhouse in the metaphysical self-help community. She has a massive series of works that explore the power of the human experience. Anatomy of the Spirit explores the practice of developing a psychic sight that enables the practitioner to read the human body and its ails and develop a healing sense. This book lays out a system of thought that considers all our mental, emotional, psychic, energetic, and physical bodies when developing a path toward healing. Empowering and thought-provoking, Anatomy of the Spirit lays a foundation of power and responsibility for the reader. While some elements can be disarming and even throw you into a rage, this book is an important piece for becoming the Divine Creator of your experience. And, therefore, a powerhouse psychic in your own right. Myss's other works include Sacred Contracts,  Entering the Castle, Energy Anatomy, and Invisible Acts of Power. Carolyn Myss is a well-known figure in the metaphysical community. Her book offers a comprehensive guide to developing psychic sight, focusing on reading the human body's energy for healing. It's a transformative read laying the foundation for power and responsibility in psychic practice.

Light on Life 

by B.K.S. Iyengar

Light on Life helps to answer the question: Why? Why bother with processes toward enlightenment, power, consciousness development, and psychic awareness? A foundational piece of work by the masterful Yogi Iyengar, who helped to bring yoga to the Western world, Light on Life explores the meaning of consciousness, the point of enlightenment, and the power of meditation. In some ways, accidentally stumbling upon more profound practices of psychic development protocols, Iyengar explains the inner dimensions of the human experience with ease and grace. This book will surely aid in deepening the experience of the inner temple and, subsequently, a practitioner's meditation practice. This foundational work by the master Yogi Iyengar explores the meaning of consciousness and the power of meditation. While not explicitly a psychic book, it offers deep insights invaluable for anyone on a spiritual and psychic development path.

The Psychic How-To Series 

by Ted Andrews

Wandering into the bookstore's metaphysical or occult sections, you will surely find many titles that begin with "How To." Many of these books are written without depth and by people with little experience. While these books are often gateways to deeper and more significant works, the Ted Andrews series stands apart. Andrews was a practicing American psychic and renowned metaphysical author for over 30 years. His books are filled to the brim with valuable context and grounded practices on many specific subjects that may be of interest to the practitioner. Rather than prattle on about the subject, Andrews dives right into how to develop these skills in practical and no-uncertain terms. Use these books to develop specific skills and specialties of interest. The link above guides you to the Llewellyn authors page and includes articles written by Andrews and links to purchase any of his works. Ted Andrews' books are a treasure trove of practical advice and grounded practices for developing specific psychic skills. His works cover everything from aura reading to past life exploration, making them essential reading for any aspiring psychic.

Applying These Resources to Your Psychic Journey

Starting or continuing your journey in psychic development can be daunting. Whether you're interested in honing your skills through our Psychic Development Course or looking to deepen your understanding through Soul Activism readings, the right resources are crucial. The metaphysical community can be complex, full of diverse perspectives and sometimes ungrounded practices. That's why it's essential to approach your psychic development with clear boundaries and a critical mind.

These book recommendations will help you build a strong foundation, equipping you with the confidence and skills to navigate the metaphysical community effectively. However, remember that this is just the beginning. Continuous learning and practice are essential to become the most potent psychic you can be. If you're ready to take your skills further, consider enrolling in my Psychic Development Course or exploring Soul Activism readings to gain deeper insights into your spiritual journey. For those looking to integrate these skills into their professional Life, my Intuitive Business Consulting services can help align your spiritual and business goals.

Ready to Begin Your Journey?

Whether you're just starting or looking to deepen your practice, these resources are a valuable starting point. Contact me if you have any questions or want to enroll in one of my courses. Together, we can unlock your full psychic potential.
