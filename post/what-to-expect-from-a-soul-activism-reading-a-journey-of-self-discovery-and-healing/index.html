What to Expect from a Soul Activism Reading: A Journey of Self-Discovery and Healing

Zan Dean
Sep 12, 2024 · 5 min read

A Soul Activism reading is a deeply transformative experience that combines the power of Akashic Records readings with intuitive, action-based insights to guide you on your journey toward healing and empowerment. Created by Zan Dean, this unique approach uncovers your natural soul gifts, explores the challenges blocking your path, and provides practical steps to realign your life. The reading is divided into three key phases: Soul Nourishment, Soul Healing, and the Divine Aligned Action Plan. Each phase plays a crucial role in helping you come into alignment with your true self and heal the energetic blocks and restrictions from both your past and present lives.

Phase 1: Soul Nourishment – Discovering Your Soul’s Purpose, Gifts, and Challenges

The first phase of a Soul Activism reading is called Soul Nourishment, which focuses on understanding the energy that your soul is made up of. This phase is about uncovering your natural energetic abilities, or soul gifts, which are inherent qualities of your energy.

In this phase, you will also explore your soul’s greatest challenges and main oppositions to your natural energy. These challenges are often the root of significant blocks that may prevent you from fully expressing your gifts in this life. Understanding these oppositions is key to working through the energetic restrictions keeping you from living in alignment with your true self.

During the Soul Nourishment phase, Zan will help you explore:

  • Your Soul’s Gifts: These are unique qualities and abilities that define how your energy functions. Understanding these gifts allows you to see how you naturally create and manifest in the world, tapping into these gifts as tools for growth.
  • Your Life’s Purpose: Through the Akashic Records, you’ll gain clarity on your soul’s purpose—the energy you are here to bring to this dimension, what will bring you satisfaction and contentment. Clarity on purpose can aid in developing a more intentional life.
  • Your Soul’s Challenges: The Soul Nourishment phase also uncovers the greatest challenges that oppose your natural energy. These are the natural opposition energies that come alongside have gifts, the dichotomist energy to your natural flow.

This phase is empowering, as it helps you understand who you are at a soul level, what your natural strengths are, and what obstacles may be holding you back.

Phase 2: Soul Healing – Awareness and Clearing of Blocks and Restrictions

The second phase of the Soul Activism reading is focused on Soul Healing. This is where the transformative work of identifying and healing your blocks and restrictions takes place. Through accessing the Akashic Records, Zan will pinpoint the specific blocks and restrictions that have been holding you back from living in alignment with your soul’s gifts and purpose.

In the Soul Healing phase, awareness is key. By gaining a clear understanding of what these blocks are, you can begin to unravel and heal them. Many of these restrictions stem from unresolved past life experiences, karmic patterns, or limiting beliefs that have carried over into your present life. However, the focus of healing is always on how these blocks are affecting your current life and how you can align with your true self in the here and now. Past life healing is valuable only in how it aids your present journey.

Soul Healing includes:

  • Identifying Energetic Blocks: Zan will help you recognize the specific blocks that are limiting your growth. These may be tied to unresolved karmic issues, emotional barriers, or long-held limiting beliefs.
  • Healing Past and Present Life Imprints: Many of the blocks that affect us today are the result of past life experiences, but healing always focuses on how we can live more aligned with our true selves in this life.
  • Awareness and Clarity: The key to healing is awareness. By understanding what these blocks are and where they come from, you begin the process of unraveling them and clearing the path for growth.

The Soul Healing phase is transformative because it provides clarity on the root causes of your current challenges, offering the opportunity to clear these blocks and move forward with greater freedom and purpose.

Phase 3: Divine Aligned Action Plan – Healing Through Aligned Action

The final phase of a Soul Activism reading is the Divine Aligned Action Plan. This is where the insights from the Soul Nourishment and Soul Healing phases are translated into actionable steps designed to help you heal and align your life with your soul’s truth.

The actions developed in this phase are not arbitrary; they are specifically designed to pinpoint the blocks and restrictions identified earlier in the reading. Aligned action is the key to healing karma, as it allows you to actively work through, and take responsibility for, the energetic imprints that have been limiting your growth. This phase is where you take the insights gained and apply them in a practical way to your everyday life.

The Divine Aligned Action Plan includes:

  • Actionable Steps for Growth: Zan will brainstorm with you to develop specific actions that directly address the blocks and challenges uncovered in the reading. These steps are designed to help you realign with your natural energy and move forward with clarity and purpose.
  • Healing Through Aligned Action: The main mechanism for developing these healing actions is a unique and powerful method that Zan has developed through her many years of experience. By taking aligned action, you actively heal the karmic patterns that have been restricting your growth.
  • Sustained Personal Growth: The action plan is designed not just to offer immediate relief but to set you on a path of sustained personal growth. By incorporating these steps into your daily life, you can continue to heal and evolve long after the reading.

The Divine Aligned Action Plan ensures that the healing process doesn’t stop at awareness. It empowers you to take the necessary steps to realign with your soul’s purpose and continue your journey of spiritual growth.

A Transformative Healing with Soul Activism Readings: Aligning with Your True Self

A Soul Activism reading is a comprehensive healing journey designed to help you uncover your soul’s gifts, heal blocks from both past and present lives, and take aligned action toward your true purpose. Walking through this two to three-hour reading, Zan compassionately assists and supports you in absorbing these truths, addressing any emotions or insights that arise along the way.

The goal of psychic healing through a Soul Activism reading is to help you come into alignment with your true self-one of the most radical things that you can do

. By addressing the challenges and blocks that have been holding you back, you can move forward with clarity, confidence, and a deeper connection to your soul’s purpose. Contact Zan Dean today to book your transformative Soul Activism reading and begin your journey toward healing and empowerment.
