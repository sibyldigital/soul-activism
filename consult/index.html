Soul Sync

Intuitive Business Consulting with Zan Dean

Build a Business That Fits Your Soul

Your business should carry your values, not compete with them. Soul Sync is intuitive business consulting for psychics, healers, and spiritually led entrepreneurs who want growth that feels as aligned as it is sustainable.

Zan Dean brings two kinds of expertise to the table: years of intuitive and Akashic Records work, and formal training in business and project management (a BA in Business and Marketing and CAPM certification through the Project Management Institute). Together you'll pair practical strategy with intuitive insight, so every decision moves your business forward and keeps it true to your purpose.

What We Work On Together

ALIGN

Mission & values

Clarify what your business is here to do, then shape your offers, pricing, and messaging around that mission.

TAP IN

Intuitive decisions

Learn to bring your intuition into business choices with confidence, and to tell guidance apart from fear or habit.

STRATEGIZE

A plan you'll follow

Turn insight into concrete, prioritized next steps for marketing, operations, and growth that fit your capacity.

OVERCOME

Blocks & challenges

Work through stuck points, from visibility and money mindset to burnout, with practical and spiritual tools.

Who It's For

  • Psychics, readers, and healers ready to grow their practice professionally
  • Spiritual entrepreneurs launching a new offer or business
  • Established business owners who want to realign with their purpose
  • Anyone who wants strategy that respects both the numbers and their intuition

How a Session Works

Connect

Reach out with a short note about your business and where you feel stuck or ready to grow.

Consult

Together we look at your goals through both a strategic and an intuitive lens, and name what wants to shift.

Take aligned action

What You'll Walk Away With

  • Practical action steps to align your business with your higher self
  • Intuitive tools for manifesting success and growth
  • Guidance to overcome challenges and make soul-centered decisions
  • A supportive, empowering space to explore your business’s spiritual potential
