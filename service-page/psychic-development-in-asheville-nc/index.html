Service Description

Our eight-week Psychic Development Course is designed to help you discover and deepen your intuitive abilities, empowering you to connect with your higher self and purpose. Through guided lessons, practical exercises, and supportive community engagement, you’ll learn how to use your psychic gifts for personal growth and collective healing. This course blends spiritual empowerment with actionable skills, transforming intuition into a powerful tool for everyday life. Whether you’re a beginner or have experience, this course offers you the structure, insight, and support to unlock your potential as a soul activist and intuitive guide.

Once you have paid in full and the course begins, no refunds will be issued if your schedule changes. However, you may retake the course in a future series. Please note, no specific dates are guaranteed for future courses.
