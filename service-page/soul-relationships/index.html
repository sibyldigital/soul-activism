Service Description

This reading focuses on learning about the dynamic soul history between you and another. Discover the soul dynamics between you and your partner, mother, friend, lover, or spouse. All relationships hold similar vibrations, which is what draws us together. This reading covers every aspect of how our souls work together, create drama and issues, and work through past life blocks and circumstances together. Learn how many lives you have lived with your friend or partner. Learn about the complexities of the relationship, and heal past life karma drama with your loved one.

This reading goes over you and your loved one's Soul Structure and how they interact together. We also cover how many lives you lived together and the context in which that past life relationship took place. You also learn the life lessons that the relationship is formatted around and any blocks and restrictions triggered by the relationship.
